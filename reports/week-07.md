## What We Learned

* **Hook Benchmarks:** High early reach (median early views: 1,027) strongly correlates with cosmic-scale themes (*Scale Breaker*) and NASA/Apollo authority anchors.
* **Retention Drivers:** Retaining viewers beyond 10 seconds (median at10: 1.319; median average percentage viewed: 78.4%) requires moving directly into conflict by beat 2.
* **Ending Drag:** Verbatim recap summaries in the final beat depress engagement share (median: 0.474); seamless verbal loops or open questions prevent drop-offs.
