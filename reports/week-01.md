### What We Learned
* **High-contrast contradiction hooks** in the first two seconds consistently outperform standard historical statements, driving superior view velocity and early retention.
* **Pacing must remain tight** immediately following the hook; flat factual setups and slow transitions bleed viewers early.
* **Flat factual endings** fail to convert views into active engagement. Videos need interactive ending formats to prompt viewers to like and comment.

---

### Next Week's Single Experiment

* **The Change:** We will tighten all post-hook setups to under 3 seconds and replace flat factual endings with either seamless loops or explicit debate prompts.
* **Measurement:** We will measure success by comparing performance against our current median metrics: raising the median engaged share above **0.471** and increasing the median likes per 100 above **3.082**.
