## What we learned

- **Scale Anomalies and Myth-Busting Dominate:** Shorts covering massive cosmic scale (*Scale Breaker*) or debunking widely known beliefs (*Space Myths, Busted*) reliably hit early reach above the channel median of 725.5 views (e.g., ep175 reached 1,435 early views; ep165 reached 1,495).
- **Niche Engineering Hooks Stall:** Highly specific mission mechanics, lab history, and small objects struggle heavily with reach (e.g., ep190 got 14 early views; ep210 got 149 early views; ep221 reached only 400 early views).
- **Strong Loop Retention Drives High Avg View Percentage:** Shorts that closed the loop back to the hook delivered retention well above the 64.83% median, highlighted by ep072 (174.91% avg viewed, 2.33 at10) and ep125 (109.19% avg viewed, 1.586 at10).
