# Week 4 review (2026-10-25)

### Trend
*   **Views:** Total channel views reached 54,822. Weekly performance peaked on October 18 with 4,615 daily views.
*   **Subscribers:** Totaled 105. The channel maintained steady growth, gaining a high of 10 subscribers on October 4.

### Top 3 Shorts
1.  **ep175 - The 93-Billion-Light-Year Paradox (1,693 views):** Strong contradiction hook regarding the age vs. width of the universe; leveraged a high early reach (1,435 views in 25 hours).
2.  **ep165 - Why Pluto Never Finished Its Orbit (1,501 views):** Statement hook using a high-interest subject (Pluto) to drive immediate retention.
3.  **ep047 - The Myth of the Moon's Dark Side (1,455 views):** Mystery/Question hook ("Half of the Moon is permanently frozen... right?") successfully challenged viewer assumptions.

### Bottom 3 Shorts
1.  **ep167 - The Alien World Buried Inside Earth (11 views):** Despite 139 hours watched, it failed to trigger the algorithm’s early reach phase.
2.  **ep208 - The Tumbling Mountain That Buzzed Earth (27 views):** Story-based hook failed to create the urgency seen in higher-performing "Near Misses."
3.  **ep203 - The Asteroid That Snuck Past Earth (111 views):** Low engagement share in the first few hours prevented broader distribution.

### What We Learned
*   **Hook Power:** Counterintuitive contradiction hooks (like the 93-billion-light-year paradox) are the strongest drivers of early reach.
*   **Retention:** Middle-beat retention is best protected by stripping technical jargon and researcher names, focusing instead on the narrative.
*   **Content Focus:** Viewers prioritize immediate physical consequences (e.g., "erase your credit cards," "smashed to pieces") over abstract distances or scale.

### Next Week's Single Experiment
*   **Change:** Prioritize "Physical Impact" hooks (burning, crashing, or physical warping) over "Scale" hooks (distance/size) and include one active viewer prompt per script.
*   **Measurement:** Success will be measured by an increase in the median Engaged Share (currently 0.546) and Comments per video.
