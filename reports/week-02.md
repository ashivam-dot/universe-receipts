## What we learned
* **Engagement dictates reach:** Videos with an engaged share above the 0.539 median consistently crossed 1,000 views (e.g., ep004 at 0.566 and ep036 at 0.599). 
* **Pacing and stakes matter early:** Drops occur when early tension is missing; ep002 suffered an at10 of 0.857, whereas top performers like ep035 maintained 1.308 at10.
* **Myth-busting drives conversions:** The series *Space Myths, Busted* produced two top performers (ep028 and ep047), netting 7 subscribers between them.
