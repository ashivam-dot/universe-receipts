## What we learned

* **Retention Drives Views:** Top-performing Shorts (>2,000 views) require high average percentage viewed metrics near or above 90% (ep356 at 94.32%, ep260 at 98.43%).
* **Engaged Share Threshold:** When engaged share falls below the channel median of 0.494 (e.g., ep244 at 0.386, ep190 at 0.429), Shorts get pulled from algorithmic feed distribution early.
* **Script Openings & Endings:** Static recaps limit rewatches; script beat 1 needs active high-stakes paradoxes/questions, while endings require seamless syntactical loops.
