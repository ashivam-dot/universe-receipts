## What We Learned
* **Seamless Loops and Mystery Drive Retention:** As identified in the median metrics (engaged share: 0.514, avg_viewed: 75.61%), scripts pairing mystery hooks with continuous payoffs consistently outperform. Episodes with high at10 retention (such as ep105 at 2.109 and ep086 at 1.771) achieved massive completion rates (157.23% and 137.58%).
* **Weak Hooks Kill Distribution:** Shorts lacking punchy, curiosity-driven opening lines (like 2rz-F-WZsXA and ep002) stall under 120 views and fail to gain subscribers due to sub-0.500 engaged shares.
* **Concept Pacing Matters:** Delivering immediate physical stakes rather than front-loading reveals maintains narrative momentum and prevents the audience drop-off noted in early log reviews.
