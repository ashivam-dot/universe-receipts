# ep250 research: The Massive Tunnels Hidden Beneath the Moon (Scale Breaker)

Researched 2026-10-29 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.6-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: The Moon's surface is a radiation-soaked graveyard of craters, but hidden beneath its basaltic plains are gargantuan cathedrals of rock. Known as lunar lava tubes, these tunnels formed billions of years ago when molten rivers cooled on the outside while the liquid interior drained away. In 2009, the Japanese Kaguya spacecraft spotted a 'skylight'—a massive hole in the Marius Hills—revealing a gateway to the underground. Because the Moon has much lower gravity than Earth, these structures can reach impossible proportions. Theoretical models suggest they can remain stable at widths up to 5 kilometers (3.1 miles), providing enough space to house an entire lunar city. These caves offer a permanent refuge from extreme surface temperature swings and micrometeorite bombardment. Recently, radar analysis of the Mare Tranquillitatis pit proved these conduits exist, potentially providing a ready-made home for future human explorers.

## Disputed or left out

- One account states the interior temperature is a constant 17 degrees Celsius (63 degrees Fahrenheit), while others only describe the environment as stable or buffered from surface extremes; a script should use the specific temperature with attribution.
- While some researchers emphasize the stability of these tubes over billions of years, others warn that areas near skylights may be dangerous and prone to further collapse; a script should balance the habitat potential with exploration risks.
