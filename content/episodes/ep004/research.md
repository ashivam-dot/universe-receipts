# ep004 research: Soviet Moon Photos on US Spy Film (Mission Files)

Researched 2026-09-30 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.5-flash-lite, gemma-4-31b-it) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: In October 1959, the Soviet Union launched Luna 3, a small probe tasked with revealing the Moon's hidden far side. Because the Moon is tidally locked, humanity had never seen the hemisphere facing away from Earth. To photograph it, Soviet engineers built an automated deep-space darkroom inside the spacecraft, using an onboard chemical processor to develop, fix, and dry the film mid-flight before scanning it with a cathode-ray tube to radio the images home. The biggest twist? The radiation-resistant 35mm film inside the camera wasn't Soviet-made—it was American film salvaged from downed US Genetrix spy balloons. The resulting grainy photos proved the far side was densely cratered and nearly devoid of dark volcanic maria, permanently altering our map of the Moon and setting the stage for modern space exploration.
