# ep245 research: The Dark Planet Being Eaten Alive (Space Got Weird)

Researched 2026-10-28 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.8-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: In 2008, astronomers discovered WASP-12b, a scorching gas giant orbiting just two million miles from its host star. It whips around the star in roughly twenty-six hours, but gravity has turned this world into a nightmare. Tidal forces pull the planet into a deformed egg shape while ripping its superheated atmosphere into space. Even stranger, Hubble observations revealed WASP-12b is pitch black, darker than fresh asphalt. Because dayside temperatures soar past 4,600 degrees Fahrenheit, atmospheric molecules are torn apart, leaving no reflective cloud decks. Instead, the planet traps over 94 percent of starlight, glowing red from sheer thermal radiation. Worst of all, its clock is speeding up. Precise transit timings show WASP-12b's year shrinks by roughly 30 milliseconds annually as tidal friction drains its orbital energy. Locked in an irreversible death spiral, this pitch-black world has only about three million years left before being utterly consumed.

## Disputed or left out

- Distance from Earth: S2, S3, and S5 cite approximately 1,400 light-years, while S7 states 870 light-years. Script should omit the distance or attribute the modern 1,400 light-year figure.
- Remaining lifetime: S1 and S4 note older estimates gave WASP-12b up to 10 million years, but recent studies refine the countdown to about 3 million years. Script should say: 'estimated between 3 and 10 million years, with recent studies pointing to just 3 million.'
- Planet radius: S1 mentions the extended atmosphere reaches nearly three times Jupiter's radius, whereas S3, S5, S6, and S7 state the planet itself is 1.8 to 2 times Jupiter's radius. Clarify that the swollen planetary body is about twice Jupiter's size while the escaping gas envelope balloons even further.
