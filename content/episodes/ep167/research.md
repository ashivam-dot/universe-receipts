# ep167 research: The Alien World Buried Inside Earth (The Universe Is Terrifying)

Researched 2026-10-17 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.8-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: 4.5 billion years ago, a Mars-sized planet named Theia smashed into our infant Earth. This wasn't a minor collision—it was a cataclysm that vaporized rock and changed our world forever. While scientists once believed the resulting debris formed the Moon over many months or years, new high-resolution simulations reveal it could have happened in just a few hours. But Theia didn’t entirely disappear into space. Two massive, continent-sized 'blobs' of alien rock are still buried 1,800 miles deep in Earth's mantle, sitting just above the core. Located beneath Africa and the Pacific Ocean, these anomalies are likely the iron-rich relics of Theia's mantle that sank during the impact. We aren't just orbiting with a moon made of debris; we are living on a planet that swallowed another world whole, with its ancient heart still hidden deep beneath our feet.

## Disputed or left out

- The angle of impact is debated; early models suggested a glancing blow, while 2016 analyses suggest a direct head-on collision (attribute to scientific shift) [S1, S2].
- The speed of Moon formation is disputed; traditional theories suggest months or years, while 2022 high-resolution simulations suggest it took only hours (attribute to new simulation data) [S2, S3].
- Theia's origin is disputed; it may have formed in a stable Lagrange point near Earth or migrated from the outer solar system (attribute to different isotopic studies) [S1, S5].
