# ep380 research: Jupiter's Invisible Magnetic Monster (Scale Breaker)

Researched 2026-11-07 by the cloud studio (gemini-3-flash-preview, gemini-3.5-flash-lite, gemini-3.6-flash, gemini-3.8-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: Invisible to human eyes, Jupiter commands the largest continuous structure in the Solar System: a colossal magnetosphere driven by liquid metallic hydrogen churning deep within its interior. Fed by volcanic eruptions from its tortured moon Io, which continuously spews sulfurous gases to form a vast plasma torus, this magnetic bubble stretches millions of kilometers sunward and trails a tail reaching all the way to Saturn's orbit. Powered not by the solar wind like Earth's, but by Jupiter's ferocious 10-hour rotation, this giant dynamo unleashes permanent polar auroras 100 times more energetic than our Northern Lights. Magnetic field lines even physically tether the planet to its large moons, burning glowing auroral footprints directly into Jupiter's atmosphere. Around this monster swirl radiation belts violent enough to fry unshielded electronics, forcing NASA's Juno spacecraft to dive through extreme polar orbits to survive.

## Disputed or left out

- Apparent size from Earth if visible: S1 states the magnetosphere would appear 5 times larger than the full Moon, S2 states it would appear two to three times the size of the Sun or Moon, and S4 states it would appear as big as the Moon. A script should qualify this by stating estimates range from the size of the Moon to several times larger.
- Magnetic field strength versus magnetic moment: S5 describes Jupiter's magnetic field as 20,000 times stronger than Earth's, whereas S1 specifies that the magnetic field is about 20 times stronger while the magnetic moment is roughly 18,000 to 20,000 times larger. A script should specify that the magnetic moment is 20,000 times Earth's.
