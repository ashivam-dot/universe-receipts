# ep109 research: The Galaxy-Sized Cosmic Ghost (The Universe Is Terrifying)

Researched 2026-10-09 by the cloud studio (gemini-3-flash-preview) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: In 2007, Dutch schoolteacher Hanny van Arkel was classifying galaxies for the online citizen science project Galaxy Zoo when she noticed a bizarre, glowing green blob. Named Hanny's Voorwerp—Dutch for Hanny's Object—the structure spans the size of the Milky Way galaxy, roughly 650 million light-years from Earth. Astronomers quickly realized this eerie cloud of oxygen gas was shining without an obvious power source. The terrifying truth: it is a ghost image, or quasar light echo. Over 100,000 years ago, a supermassive black hole in neighboring galaxy IC 2497 ignited a brilliant quasar, blasting a beam of extreme radiation across intergalactic space. Although that monstrous engine has long since starved and switched off, its light beam is still traveling across deep space, illuminating the massive gas streamer in glowing green light. Gas flows from the dead quasar's host galaxy are now sparking new star birth within the cosmic ghost.

## Disputed or left out

- Discovery year: S1, S6, and S7 state the object was discovered in 2007, while S5 states 2008. Scripts should say 'around 2007' or leave the specific discovery year out.
- Nature of the hole: S1 and S3 describe a physical opening possibly punched by a particle jet, whereas S2 suggests it may be an optical illusion caused by an obscuring shadow cast in the quasar beam. Scripts should describe it as an apparent hole or shadow.
- Magnitude of quasar dimming: Early observations suggested the quasar shut down completely by multiple orders of magnitude (S1, S3, S4, S6), while later X-ray data revealed the core is partially obscured by dust and dropped in brightness by a factor of 50 (S5, S6). Scripts should state the core dropped dramatically in brightness rather than citing an absolute shutdown.
