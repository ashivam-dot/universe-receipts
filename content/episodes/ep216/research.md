# ep216 research: The Escape Velocity Myth (Space Myths, Busted)

Researched 2026-10-23 by the cloud studio (gemini-3-flash-preview, gemini-3.6-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: You do not need to blast off at 25,000 miles per hour to escape Earth's gravity. That terrifying speed limit, known as escape velocity, is actually just a shortcut for a single unpowered burst. If you fire a projectile and let gravity do all the work, yes, you need 11.2 kilometers per second to coast into the cosmos forever. But rockets are not cannonballs. By keeping their engines burning, they can climb at a leisurely walking pace and still slip away completely. Escape velocity is not a speed limit you must break; it is merely an energy bill. And thanks to continuous thrust, you do not have to pay it all at once.

## Disputed or left out

- Slight variations in decimal places for constants like G and planetary mass values across different sources; scripts should use standard accepted rounded figures like 11.2 km/s.
