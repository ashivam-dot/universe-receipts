Researched 2026-09-30 by the cloud studio (gemini-3-flash-preview, gemini-3.5-flash-lite, gemma-4-31b-it) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: In 1957, the Soviet launch of Sputnik 1 triggered a 'Sputnik crisis' of national panic in the United States. President Dwight D. Eisenhower realized that military infighting was losing the Space Race and moved to create a unified civilian agency. Instead of building from scratch, the government rebranded the 43-year-old National Advisory Committee for Aeronautics (NACA). NASA officially opened on October 1, 1958, inheriting 8,000 employees, three major research laboratories, and a $100 million budget. The agency's early success relied on Wernher von Braun, a former Nazi scientist who had developed the V-2 rocket during World War II. Despite early setbacks, like the televised fireball of the Vanguard TV3 rocket, the agency successfully launched Explorer 1 into orbit by early 1958. Operating initially from a temporary headquarters in the Dolley Madison House, this rebranded agency transformed from a research group into the organization that would land humans on the moon just a decade later.

## Disputed or left out

- The exact age of NACA at the time of transition is stated as 43 years in S4 but 46 years in S1; the script should focus on the founding year of 1915 to remain accurate.
- The launch date of Explorer 1 is listed as February 1, 1958, in S2, while S1, S3, and S7 state January 31, 1958; the script should use January 31 as the primary date according to most accounts.
