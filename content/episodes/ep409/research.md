# ep409 research: The Space Detector Wider Than the Sun (Scale Breaker)

Researched 2026-11-13 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.6-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: Imagine a telescope in space so massive its arms stretch 1.6 million miles across the void—wider than the Sun itself. This is LISA, the Laser Interferometer Space Antenna, a planned European Space Agency mission scheduled to launch in 2035 aboard an Ariane 6 rocket. Composed of three spacecraft flying in a giant triangular formation following Earth, LISA acts as the first dedicated space-based gravitational-wave observatory. As invisible ripples in spacetime—generated by colliding supermassive black holes and dead stars—wash over the solar system, they subtly squeeze and stretch the distance between the spacecraft. By bouncing lasers between free-floating gold-platinum cubes, LISA will measure these cosmic tremors with a jaw-dropping precision smaller than the width of a single helium atom. By breaking free from the seismic noise of Earth, this colossal space antenna will open an entirely new window to listen to the dark universe.
