# ep114 research: The Cosmic Signal Stronger Than the Sun (Scale Breaker)

Researched 2026-10-09 by the cloud studio (gemini-3-flash-preview, gemini-3.5-flash, gemini-3.5-flash-lite) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: In 2007, astronomers searching through archival data from Australia's Parkes Observatory discovered a mind-bending cosmic mystery: the first Fast Radio Burst (FRB). These millisecond-long flashes of deep-space radio waves are incredibly powerful, discharging as much energy in a fraction of a second as our Sun does in days. Yet, because they travel across billions of light-years, their signals are incredibly weak by the time they reach Earth—one thousand times fainter than a cell phone signal on the Moon! Scientists estimate that up to ten thousand of these bursts happen across the sky every single day. While they were long thought to be one-off, cataclysmic destructions, the discovery of repeating burst FRB 121102 shattered that theory. Even more shocking, researchers recently found 'ultra-fast' radio bursts from this same source that last a mere ten millionths of a second, revealing a hidden, hyper-rapid universe.

## Disputed or left out

- The exact recording date of the first FRB: S1 states it occurred on July 24, 2001, whereas S4 states it occurred on August 24, 2001. A script should state the year (2001) without specifying the month to avoid this conflict.
- The duration of the first FRB: S1 states it was less than 5 milliseconds in duration, while S4 describes it as a five-millisecond burst. A script should generalize it as lasting approximately five milliseconds.
- Whether FRB 121102 was the only repeating source at the time: S5 states it is the only known repeating source, whereas S1 and S6 note that many other repeating FRBs have since been found. A script should clarify that it was the first repeating source discovered, not the only one.
