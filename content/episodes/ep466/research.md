# ep466 research: The Eerie Hum Voyager 1 Found in Interstellar Space (The Universe Is Terrifying)

Researched 2026-11-22 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.6-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: Launched in 1977, Voyager 1 completed flybys of Jupiter, Saturn, and Titan before soaring toward the edge of our solar system. On August 25, 2012, it became the first human-made object to cross the heliopause and enter interstellar space. Because its primary plasma sensor failed back in 1980, scientists had to wait for solar outbursts to measure the surrounding interstellar medium. A massive solar eruption in 2012 finally reached the probe in 2013, vibrating the interstellar plasma around it like a violin string and revealing plasma tens of times denser than inside the solar bubble. Beyond these rare solar shocks, Voyager 1 detected a persistent, faint low-frequency hum vibrating through the cosmic gas—the eerie, continuous soundtrack of interstellar space itself. Powered by nuclear generators, Voyager 1 continues transmitting data across light-days of space, braving the deep vacuum alone.
