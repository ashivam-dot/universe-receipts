# ep117 research: The Galaxy With a Missing Heart (The Universe Is Terrifying)

Researched 2026-10-10 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.6-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: At the center of galaxy cluster Abell 2261 sits A2261-BCG, an elliptical monster galaxy spanning one million light-years across—ten times the size of the Milky Way. Given its colossal mass, astronomers calculated its core should harbor an absolute titan of a supermassive black hole weighing between 3 billion and 100 billion solar masses. But when telescopes looked inside, they found an eerie cosmic ghost town. The galaxy possesses a bloated, diffuse core 10,000 light-years across with no central peak of starlight. Deeper observations by the Hubble Space Telescope and Chandra X-ray Observatory failed to detect any telltale X-rays from an active black hole. The prime suspect? An ancient galactic collision where two supermassive black holes merged, generating lopsided gravitational waves that acted like a cosmic rocket engine, completely ejecting the multi-billion-solar-mass monster out of its host galaxy into intergalactic space.
