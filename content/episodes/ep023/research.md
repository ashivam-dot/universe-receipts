# ep023 research: The Quasar in Your Backyard Telescope (The Universe Is Terrifying)

Researched 2026-10-02 by the cloud studio (gemini-3-flash-preview, gemini-3.7-flash, gemini-3.8-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: In 1963, astronomers were baffled by an enigmatic, star-like object cataloged as 3C 273. Its spectrum defied classification, showing bizarre, unidentifiable lines. Then astronomer Maarten Schmidt realized the truth: those lines were normal hydrogen, but stretched massively by a staggering redshift. 3C 273 was flying away from Earth at a fraction of the speed of light, placing it an astonishing 2.4 billion light-years away. To be visible from that unimaginable distance, this point of light had to be trillions of times brighter than the Sun. Scientists soon discovered it was the first identified quasar, powered by a colossal supermassive black hole consuming material at its galactic core, shooting out a 200,000-light-year jet, and hiding in plain sight in the constellation Virgo for amateur telescopes to spot.
