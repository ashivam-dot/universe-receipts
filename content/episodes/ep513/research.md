Researched 2026-11-27 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.5-flash-lite, gemini-3.6-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: Deep in the Sahara Desert of Mauritania lies the Richat Structure, a striking 25-mile-wide formation of concentric rings known as the Eye of Africa. Visible from orbit, scientists originally believed this immense circular landmark was formed by a violent meteor impact. However, extensive geological studies proved it is actually a natural dome created by underground magma pushing upward millions of years ago, which later eroded into dramatic ridges and valleys as wind and water carved away alternating layers of hard and soft rock. Because of its distinctive circular shape, fringe theorists frequently claim the site is the lost city of Atlantis described by Plato. Yet, mainstream scholars emphasize that Plato invented Atlantis as a fictional moral fable. Furthermore, archaeological surveys have found zero evidence of an ancient city or canals, and its location deep inside a desert contradicts ancient accounts of an ocean-bound island that sank into the sea.

## Disputed or left out

- Fringe claims proposing that the Richat Structure is Atlantis are unsupported by archaeology and geology; the script should attribute this strictly as an unsubstantiated fringe theory.
- The early impact crater hypothesis was disproven after tests showed no evidence of shock metamorphism; the script should frame the impact origin as an outdated historical belief.
