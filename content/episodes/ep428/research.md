# ep428 research: Why Venus Glows With a Ring of Fire (Space Got Weird)

Researched 2026-11-16 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.5-flash-lite, gemini-3.6-flash, gemini-3.8-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: In late 1610, Galileo Galilei aimed his new telescope at Venus and discovered something that shattered ancient astronomy: Venus changed shape just like the Moon. Because the prevailing Ptolemaic model claimed Venus never passed behind the Sun, seeing a full sequence of phases proved the Earth-centered universe was wrong. Terrified of being scooped before confirming his work, Galileo hid his discovery inside a Latin anagram sent to Johannes Kepler: 'Haec immatura a me iam frustra leguntur o.y.' When decoded, it revealed that the mother of love imitates the shapes of Cynthia. Over a century later, during the 1761 transit of Venus across the Sun, astronomers noticed another jaw-dropping effect: as Venus touched the Sun's edge, sunlight refracted through its thick atmosphere, bending light into a glowing ring around the dark planet. This celestial halo gave humanity its very first proof that another planet possessed an atmosphere.
