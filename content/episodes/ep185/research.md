# ep185 research: Why Space Will NEVER Make You Explode (Space Myths, Busted)

Researched 2026-10-18 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.5-flash-lite, gemini-3.7-flash, gemini-3.8-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: Movies show people exploding in space, but you won’t burst. Your skin is tough enough to contain the pressure, and your blood won’t boil. You also won’t freeze instantly because heat loss is slow in a vacuum. Instead, you’ll experience ebullism: gas bubbles forming in your fluids that can bloat you to twice your size. The real danger is hypoxia. Your lungs actually strip oxygen from your blood, leaving you with just 15 seconds of consciousness. NASA technician Jim LeBlanc survived a 1960s vacuum chamber accident. His last memory before passing out was the saliva on his tongue beginning to bubble. He was rescued and recovered, proving you have roughly 90 seconds to be saved before it’s fatal.
