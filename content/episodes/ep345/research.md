# ep345 research: The Asteroid That Bakes Like a Comet (Space Got Weird)

Researched 2026-11-04 by the cloud studio (gemini-3-flash-preview, gemini-3.5-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: Asteroid 3200 Phaethon is a weird space rock that behaves like a comet. Discovered in 1983, it has an extreme orbit bringing it closer to the Sun than Mercury, heating its surface to over 700 degrees Celsius. Scientists long believed solar heat blasted dust off the asteroid to create the famous annual Geminid meteor shower. However, recent NASA data from solar observatories revealed a stunning twist: Phaethon's tail is not made of dust at all, but of sodium gas. Because gas cannot form meteor showers, the true origin of the Geminids remains a mystery. JAXA plans to launch the DESTINY+ mission to fly by Phaethon and finally solve the puzzle of this rocky interloper.
