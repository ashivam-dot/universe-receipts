# ep013 research: The Mountain Where You Cannot See the Peak (Scale Breaker)

Researched 2026-10-01 by the cloud studio (gemini-3-flash-preview) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: On Mars sits Olympus Mons, the largest volcano in the solar system. It towers roughly three times the height of Mount Everest and spreads about as wide as the state of Arizona. Yet if you stood on its slopes, you wouldn't even know you were on a mountain. Because its shield volcano profile has an average slope of just five percent, the ground appears as a gently sloping plain stretching to a horizon a mere three kilometers away. Its summit, featuring six nested calderas up to three kilometers deep, remains completely out of view. Instead of a soaring peak, visitors would experience an immense regional landscape, complete with a basal cliff reaching up to nine kilometers high in places.
