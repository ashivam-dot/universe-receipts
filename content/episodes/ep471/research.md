# ep471 research: The Giant Asteroid That Sneaked Past Earth (Near Misses)

Researched 2026-11-21 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.6-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: In January 2018, a sub-kilometer asteroid known as 2018 AH zipped past Earth at a distance of roughly 184,500 miles, which is closer than the Moon. Measuring around 100 to 190 meters in diameter—making it comparable in size to the Washington Monument—it was the largest asteroid to pass that close to Earth since 2002 JE9 in 1971. Despite its massive size and proximity, astronomers completely missed it before its closest approach. It was only discovered two days later, on January 4, 2018, by the ATLAS survey on Mauna Loa. The asteroid had approached undetected from the direction of the Sun following perihelion, remaining too dim for standard survey detection until it was gone.
