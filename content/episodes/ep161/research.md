# ep161 research: The Fastest Craft Ever Built Plunges Into the Sun (Scale Breaker)

Researched 2026-10-17 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.8-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: Launched in 2018, NASA's Parker Solar Probe became the fastest human-made object in history, plunging directly through the blistering outer atmosphere of the Sun, known as the corona. Designed to endure extreme solar radiation, the spacecraft utilizes a massive 4.5-inch-thick carbon-composite heat shield that can withstand temperatures reaching roughly 2,500 degrees Fahrenheit, while keeping its sensitive instruments at a cool 85 degrees. By executing seven Venus gravity assists over a seven-year span to shrink its orbit, the probe reached a blistering heliocentric speed of approximately 430,000 miles per hour at its closest approach. This historic mission finally allows scientists to directly sample solar wind and solve decades-old mysteries regarding why the Sun's corona is vastly hotter than its actual surface.
