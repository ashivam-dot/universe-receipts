# ep071 research: The 50/50 Chance of a Galactic Crash (The Universe Is Terrifying)

Researched 2026-10-06 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.6-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: For over a century, astronomers warned that the Milky Way was on an inescapable collision course with the Andromeda galaxy. This 'galactic smashup' was once considered as inevitable as death and taxes. However, a groundbreaking 2025 study using the latest data from the Hubble and Gaia space telescopes has flipped the script. By running up to 100,000 simulations that include the gravitational pull of nearby dwarf galaxies, researchers found the chance of a merger within the next 10 billion years is actually just a 50/50 coin flip. Our neighbor, the Large Magellanic Cloud, is acting like a cosmic bodyguard, pulling the Milky Way away from the impact zone. While a head-on crash has only a 2% chance of happening in the next 5 billion years, the Milky Way is still on track to swallow the Large Magellanic Cloud much sooner. Even if the big crash eventually happens, the stars are so far apart they likely won't even touch.

## Disputed or left out

- The exact number of simulations: Press releases state 100,000 simulations (S1, S2, S3), while the journal abstract specifies 50,000 for the primary model (S5). The script should refer to 'up to 100,000 simulations.'
- The timeline for collision: Historical estimates cited 3.75 billion years (S7), while newer data suggests a range of 4.5 to 5 billion years (S1, S3, S6). The script should use the newer 4.5-5 billion year window.
