# ep078 research: The Bubble That Could Erase the Universe (The Universe Is Terrifying)

Researched 2026-10-08 by the cloud studio (gemini-3.7-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: Our universe might be living on borrowed time in a false vacuum, a metastable state of local stability that could suddenly and violently collapse. If a pocket of space were to reach a lower energy state through quantum tunneling, it would form a bubble of true vacuum. Expanding at nearly the speed of light, this bubble would instantly rewrite the laws of physics, alter fundamental constants, and obliterate everything in its path with zero warning. While theoretical calculations suggest our vacuum is extraordinarily long-lived, recent experiments using quantum computers have successfully simulated the formation and interaction of these universe-destroying bubbles in the lab.

## Disputed or left out

- Exact estimates of the vacuum lifetime range from 10^65 years to over 10^100 or 10^790 years depending on the specific papers and models cited; scripts should generalize that the lifetime is unimaginably long.
- Whether the universe's vacuum is strictly metastable, absolutely stable, or unstable depends heavily on unknown high-energy physics beyond the Standard Model and precise top quark mass measurements; scripts should present metastability as a leading current hypothesis.
