# ep410 research: The Full Moon Madness Myth Busted (Space Myths, Busted)

Researched 2026-11-13 by the cloud studio (gemini-3-flash-preview, gemini-3.5-flash-lite, gemini-3.6-flash) from the sources below, read in full by the studio. Only claims backed by two or more independent sites were allowed into the script.

Story: For centuries, folklore has claimed that a full Moon sparks spikes in violent crime, emergency room visits, homicides, and sudden births. Many police officers and hospital workers still believe in this lunar effect today. However, extensive literature reviews and meta-analyses of decades of data across multiple countries show zero correlation between lunar phases and human behavior or biology. Major studies confirm the Moon has no effect on human birth rates, emergency room admissions, suicides, or homicides. In famous cases claiming full Moon hospital spikes, re-analysis revealed serious statistical errors, as admission rates on full Moon days were identical to several non-full Moon days. Apparent spikes in crime or accidents are actually driven by day-of-the-week trends like weekend traffic. While marine animals like corals rely on moonlight to time spawning, human body fluid is bounded and unaffected by lunar gravity. The lunar effect is purely a myth sustained by cognitive bias.
